Compliance & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026  |  Applies to: DeskMosaic and DeskMosaic: Student Edition

1. Core Principle: Local & Private by Design

DeskMosaic and DeskMosaic: Student Edition are offline-first desktop applications built for Windows. We believe your desktop workspace, habits, and schedules are strictly your own business. The software operates independently on your local machine without mandatory user account creation or background telemetry.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identity Data: We do not ask for or collect names, physical addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not log keystrokes, application usage hours, widget configurations, or screen contents.
  • No Cloud Harvesting: Your local notes, timers, audio settings, and files are never uploaded to remote servers.

3. Local Storage & Device Communication

All settings, custom palettes, workspace layouts, and presets are saved locally on your computer inside standard Windows application data directories.

For features utilizing local network data transfer (such as mobile drop or local Wi-Fi clipboard synchronization in the Student Edition), communications occur strictly across your own local area network (LAN). Data is transmitted directly peer-to-peer between your authorized devices and is never routed through external cloud infrastructure.

4. Payments & Store Licensing

All application purchases, license verifications, downloads, and refund inquiries are processed exclusively through the Microsoft Store and your personal Microsoft Account. DeskMosaic Software never receives, processes, or retains credit card numbers, banking information, or billing records.

5. Support & Inquiries

If you contact support directly via email, your email address and correspondence will be used solely to answer your questions and diagnose technical issues. We will never sell, lease, or share your contact info.

For technical inquiries or policy questions, contact us at: deskmosaic.apps@gmail.com
